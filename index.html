The Tradition

A fire that brings people together

For years now, the homestead in Black Diamond has hosted an annual whole-hog roast. Smoke rises through the firs, the spit turns, and friends gather around the firepit with plates that never stay empty for long.

It's not a formal event. It's the best kind of day: casual, a little wild, full of good food and better company. Tractor in the background. Drink station stocked. Potluck tables overflowing. Kids and dogs and stories that get better every year.

— The Taranowski Homestead

What to Expect

The shape of the day

01

The Fire

Whole hog over open fire or pit. Smoke starts early. The main event takes its time — and that's the point.

02

The Spread

Pulled pork, crackling, sauces. Potluck sides that somehow always include the best of everything. Drink station stocked.

03

The Vibe

Lawn chairs. Firepit stories. Badminton if the mood hits. Kids, dogs, and the kind of lingering that only happens when nobody is watching the clock.

04

The Place

Private homestead in Black Diamond. Forest edge. Tractor. Room to breathe. Bring what you want to share — or just show up hungry.
